**OFFICE MEMORANDUM**

**Subject: Amendment to Manual for Procurement of Consultancy & Other Services (MfPCOS), 2017.**

Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade has vide Gazette Notification No. G.S.R. 127(E) dated 19.02.2019 amended the definition of "Startup." It has, therefore, been decided to partially modify para 1.9.1(ix) of **Manual for Procurement of Consultancy & Other Services, 2017** as under:
